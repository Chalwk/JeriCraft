⚔️ **START HERE**
1. **Read the rules** (link above)
2. **Link Discord** in-game: `/discord link`
3. **Claim your starter kit**: `/kit` to view, then `/kit <name>` to redeem
4. **Pick a job**: `/jobs` to browse and join a profession
5. **Head to Survival**: `/survival` - required for `/f` and `/rt` commands
6. **Join or create a faction**: `/f join <name>` or `/f create <name>` *(Survival only)*
7. **Visit spawn**: `/spawn` (castle hub with NPC merchants)
8. **Explore the wild**: `/rt` for a random wilderness spot *(Survival only)*
9. **Lost?** `/f home` *(Survival only)* or `/spawn` to return safely

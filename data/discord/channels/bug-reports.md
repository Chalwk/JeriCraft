Found a bug, glitch, or issue on the server? You have two options:

**1.** Post in this forum (preferred for quick reports).
**2.** Or, if you prefer a structured approach, use the [Bug Report Form](https://github.com/Chalwk/JeriCraft/issues/new?template=bug-report.yaml) on GitHub.

Please keep it to one issue per post/thread. If you have multiple problems, make separate reports.

**Please include:**
- Minecraft username
- Date and time (if known)
- What the bug/problem is
- Location (coordinates, biome, world, nearby structures, Faction claim status if relevant)
- Steps to reproduce
- Expected behavior
- Client version and any mods/resource packs
- Screenshots or video if possible

If you are not sure whether it is a bug or just normal Minecraft/PaperMC behaviour, still report it. Just mention that you are unsure.

For exploits, duplication bugs, or anything that could be abused, do not post details publicly - DM a staff member instead.

Please avoid DMing staff bug reports unless it is private or urgent. Posting here keeps everything trackable and may help others!

Everyone is welcome to jump in and share solutions.

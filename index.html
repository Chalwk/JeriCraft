Welcome to JeriCraft

JeriCraft is a Medieval-themed SMP/RPG Factions server for Minecraft Java Edition, inspired by Late Middle Ages Northern Europe. Claim land, build fortresses, forge alliances, and wage war in a player-driven world.

Choose from 24+ Jobs, level mcMMO skills, trade through Chest Shops and NPC merchants, and survive four seasons with adaptive levelled mobs. Read more about JeriCraft →
